Magda's Law

A service animal's status should follow the legitimate animal and handler relationship, regardless of which Canadian law is being applied.

A proposal to amend the service animal definition in Criminal Code s. 445.01(4), building on Quanto's Law.

Read the proposalReview Draft 9

The recognition gap

Same animal. Different definitions.

Ontario law can recognize an owner trained service animal through disability related need and qualifying health professional documentation. The specialized federal offence in Criminal Code s. 445.01 uses a narrower definition requiring written certification of training by a professional service animal institution.

An animal may therefore qualify under Ontario law while the federal service animal offence may be unavailable for harm to that same animal. Ontario protections still apply.

The policy question

If an animal is a lawful service animal immediately before an attack, why should federal criminal law be capable of treating that same animal differently solely because the handler trained the animal?

The proposed change

Carry legitimate recognition forward.

01 · RECOGNIZE

Established status

Recognize service animal status established under applicable provincial or territorial law.

02 · PRESERVE

Existing route

Keep the existing professional institution training route to federal recognition.

03 · REVIEW

Precise drafting

Have Parliamentary counsel settle the statutory wording after reviewing provincial and territorial regimes.

This is a policy drafting concept. It does not propose that self declaration alone establish service animal status.

The animal behind the proposal

Meet Magda.

Magda is an owner trained service animal whose work includes alerting her handler when he falls and helping him navigate daily life. Following a serious spinal injury in December 2025, she spent approximately six months recovering. She regained mobility and returned to work, though weakness remains in her hindquarters.

Her experience shows why recognition matters when injury interrupts the support a disabled person relies upon. Magda's case illustrates the policy gap; this proposal asks Parliament to address the definition, not to decide liability in an individual incident.

Strength and service

Magda is a real working animal who endured an attack, recovered, and returned to her role. Her story gives this proposal its name and purpose.
